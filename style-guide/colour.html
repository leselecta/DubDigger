Colour

One accent.

A near-black ground, four greys below white, four hairline weights, and a single accent. There is no second hue anywhere on the site, and spending the one more widely is exactly what would stop it working.

The ramp has a floor, and it is a contrast floor. A new grey has to clear 4.5:1 or it is not a grey, it is a bug. The bottom two used to be #787878 and #6a6a6a; the latter came to 3.75:1 while carrying every column heading, count, year and the whole footer.

The three quiet greys are 16 apart in hex. That even step is what makes them read as a deliberate ramp rather than as three shades that drifted. Move one and move the others with it.

What each grey carries
Token Class Job
--color-ink-strong text-ink-strong Headlines, the three figures, the active tab, the contact address. The one thing on screen you are meant to land on.
--color-ink text-ink Body default, set on <body>. Field values, chip labels, nav cells, the type column in results.
--color-ink-muted text-ink-muted Prose. Every text-lead and text-body paragraph, the absence rows, the "low" grade, the search icon at rest.
--color-ink-dim text-ink-dim Role strings under a name, resting tab labels, a chip with no page behind it.
--color-ink-faint text-ink-faint Column headings, counts, years, field labels, the "(Beta)" note, the whole footer, the grey clause after a relevance grade.

Hairlines

Hairlines, not borders. Separation without drawing a box. All four are white at low alpha rather than a grey, so they sit on whatever is behind them.

hairline — 0.12 — structural: band tops, list heads, the header and footer rules
hairline-soft — 0.08 — between rows of a list, so the head reads heavier than the rows
edge — 0.22 — decoration only, 1.86:1. Chips at rest, and nothing that is a control boundary
edge-strong — 0.35 — 3.08:1. Every control boundary: the header field, ghost buttons, the dialog

Anything that is the boundary of a control wears edge-strong. At 0.22 alpha edge is 1.86:1, under the 3:1 a control boundary has to clear — and on a field the boundary is the whole of what says where to click.

link-rule marks anything that pivots, at whatever size the type is: border-bottom: 1px solid rgb(255 255 255 / 0.25). With one accent and no room to spend it, that hairline is how a link is told apart from the text beside it.

The accent

#6fcabd, 10.48:1 as type on the ground and the same ratio the other way round, which is why bg-accent text-bg is a legal pairing for the current nav cell and the skip link.

The accent covers the top two grades, not just the top one. This column is scanned rather than read, and what a digger is scanning for is the near half of the scale. Two of five since the fifth step arrived on 2026-08-25: three would be over half the scale, which is where a mark stops marking. Below the boundary the three quiet greys take one step each, in the order the ramp already runs, which is why the scale is five words and not six.

As Where Why it earns it
type Eyebrow Names what you are looking at, which the big name cannot: "Chain Reaction" is both a label and two artists.
type The two headline stops The full stops in "Dig the Extended Scene." and "Dub Techno First." Also the wordmark's dot and the Info / Core headings.

type "Nothing found" The one heading that has to be read rather than counted past. It spends colour the dropped headline just freed.
type The three-count line Releases · collaborators · labels, under an entity name. The separators are ink-strong.
ground Current nav cell, skip link bg-accent text-bg at 10.48:1. A filled cell is legible from the far side of the row.
border Hero search field It wears the accent from the start, because it is why the home page exists.
state Focus ring, chip hover, header field focus, drawer current row Reaching for something is the other thing the colour is allowed to say.
state ::selection Accent ground, bg type.

Ten places, in two categories, and the categories are the test. As type the accent says what a thing is; as a ground, a border or a state it says what you are on or reaching for. A new use is either a step in the near half of a scale or a thing being reached for, or it is dilution. Counted and settled 2026-08-16.

States

Focus

Tab to me 
a:focus-visible, input:focus-visible, button:focus-visible, summary:focus-visible {
  outline: 2px solid var(--color-accent);
  outline-offset: 3px;
}

summary is in the list on purpose. The phone menu is one, and it is the only control on the site that is neither an anchor nor a button. Left out, it fell back to whatever ring the browser draws — the one focus state the design did not choose.

The hero field is the one exception, and it marks focus with an inset shadow instead. A ring sits 3px outside the box, and the hero is the widest thing on the home page.
